How Cogniroute works

A simple three-step concept for driver awareness.

01 — Detect

Sense eye-blink activity

The IR sensor provides readings to the device controller.

02 — Evaluate

Check the trigger

The controller checks readings against the prototype's configured threshold.

03 — Alert

Notify the driver

The buzzer and LEDs provide an alert when the configured condition is met.

Important: Cogniroute is a developing prototype, not a certified medical or automotive safety device. It cannot guarantee drowsiness detection or prevent accidents. Drivers should stop and rest whenever they feel sleepy.
